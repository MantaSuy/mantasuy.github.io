Critter Merge: Cute Animals – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Critter Merge: Cute Animals ("the app", formerly Animal 2048) is an animal merge puzzle game for children and families, published by developer Manta Suy ("we", "us") (Android, package com.manny.game2048). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Short version: Critter Merge does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no ads, no accounts, and no analytics. An optional parent-gated one-time in-app purchase uses Google Play Billing only.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, age, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) or any other personal information, and it does not send personal information to us.

Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a few game details in its private storage on your device:

  • the current games (tile positions, score, undo and hint counts) and best scores
  • which animals you have collected, stars earned and whether today's daily challenge is done
  • your settings (music, sound effects, gentle buzz, calm mode, chosen theme and game mode)
  • whether the optional Unlock Everything purchase has been unlocked on this device (also confirmed with Google Play when you restore purchases)

This information never leaves the app through us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Optional in-app purchase (Google Play Billing)

Critter Merge offers one optional one-time purchase ("Unlock Everything") that unlocks themes and extra undo/hints. It is placed behind a parent check. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment card details; Google handles the transaction under Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The app uses network access only to talk to Google Play for product details, purchase, and restore. It does not use the network for ads, analytics, tracking, or any other purpose.

No advertising

The app contains no advertising and no ad SDKs, and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's saved progress in your personal backup (managed by Google under your Google account settings), so your progress can be restored on a new phone. We cannot see or access these backups. You can turn device backup off in Android Settings.

Children

Critter Merge is designed for children and families. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children. It contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs, no chat or user-generated content, and no links to websites or other apps. The only optional purchase is a one-time unlock gated behind a parent check. We follow Google Play's Families Policy.

Data sharing and selling

We do not share, sell or rent any information, because we do not have any personal information from you.

Permissions

The app does not request special Android permissions beyond what Google Play Billing needs to complete an optional purchase (network access to Google Play only). Short vibration "ticks" on merges (which can be turned off in the app's settings) use Android's standard touch-feedback feature.

Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Purchase records are handled by Google Play.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds a feature that needs data), we will update this page and change the dates above before that version is released.
