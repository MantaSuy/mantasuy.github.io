Critter Merge: Cute Animals – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026 (version 3.3)

Critter Merge: Cute Animals ("the app", formerly Animal 2048) is an animal merge puzzle game for children and families, published by developer Manta Suy ("we", "us") (Android, package com.manny.game2048). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Short version: We do not collect any personal information ourselves: no accounts, no sign-in, no servers of our own and no analytics. Starting with version 3.3 the app shows optional, child-directed rewarded video ads from Google AdMob and offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play, which is always behind a parent check.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, age, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not send personal information to us.

Information stored only on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves game details in its private storage on your device:

  • the current games (tile positions, score, undo, hint and continue counts) and best scores
  • collected animals, stars, coins, daily reward streak and daily challenge status
  • your settings (music, sound effects, gentle buzz, calm mode, chosen animals, scenery theme and game mode)
  • which optional purchases and coin-unlocked themes you own on this device (purchases are also confirmed with Google Play)

This information never leaves the app through us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app (coins are lost when you do this).

Advertising (Google AdMob, rewarded ads only)

The app contains rewarded video ads only, from Google AdMob (Google LLC). There are no banner ads and no ads between games. A rewarded ad is shown only when the player chooses to watch one, for example to continue after "game over", to double a daily reward, or to get free coins.

  • All ad requests are tagged as child-directed (and under the age of consent), and ad content is limited to the G rating. Under these settings Google serves only non-personalized ads, with no interest-based advertising or remarketing.
  • To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process: device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID), IP address and an approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), ad interaction data, and diagnostic/crash data related to ads.
  • The data is sent directly to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not receive it. Google's use of it is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

The "Remove Ads" purchase marks the app as ad-free for any future non-rewarded ads. Optional rewarded ads stay available because the player chooses to watch them.

Consent (Google User Messaging Platform)

When the app starts, it checks with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) whether a consent message is needed in your region (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some U.S. states). Because the app is child-directed, Google treats requests as coming from users under the age of consent. Ads are only requested after this check. To reset your choices, clear the app's data in Android Settings or contact us.

Optional in-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers optional purchases: Unlock Everything, Remove Ads (with bonus coins), a Theme Pack and coin packs. Every purchase and restore is behind a parent check. Purchases are processed by Google Play (Google LLC); we never receive your card or payment details. To deliver and restore purchases, the app receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play (product ID, purchase token and order status) and keeps a record of owned items on your device only. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Links to other apps

The "More Games" screen lists our other games. Tapping one first shows a parent check, then opens the Google Play Store, which is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's saved progress in your personal backup (managed by Google under your Google account settings). We cannot see or access these backups.

Children

Critter Merge is designed for children and families and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We do not collect personal information from children. Ads are child-directed, G-rated, non-personalized and optional (rewarded only). Purchases and external links are behind a parent check. There is no chat or user-generated content.

Your choices

  • The game works fully offline. When your device is offline, no ads are loaded and nothing is sent to Google.
  • You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • You can delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app uses network access (for Google Play Billing and Google AdMob) and the Advertising ID permission used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Short vibration "ticks" use Android's standard touch-feedback feature and can be turned off in the app.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal information. The only data that leaves the app is what the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing send directly to Google, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and change the dates above before that version is released.
