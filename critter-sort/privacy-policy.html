Critter Sort – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

Critter Sort ("the app") is an animal sorting puzzle game published by developer Manta Suy ("we", "us"), Philippines (package com.mantasuy.crittersort). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information ourselves. We have no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. Your level progress, stars, coins, shop items, daily reward streak, and settings are saved only on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. These include full-screen ads between some levels and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch (for example to double coins or a daily gift, or to get an extra undo, hint, or tube). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • ad interaction data (ad views, clicks) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads

Personalized ads are shown only subject to your consent where required (collected through Google UMP); otherwise non-personalized ads may be shown. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Consent: In regions where it is required (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and some U.S. states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message (Google User Messaging Platform, UMP) when you open it. To change your choice later, open Settings > Privacy options in the game (shown where required), or clear the app's data (Settings > Apps > Critter Sort > Storage) and the consent message will be shown again, or contact us.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Offline play: The game works fully offline. When your device is offline, no ads are loaded.

Children

Critter Sort is meant for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences with parental guidance (PG) or lower.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share personal information. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is sent to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.
