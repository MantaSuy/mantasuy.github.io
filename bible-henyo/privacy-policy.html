Bible Henyo – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026 · Last updated: October 23, 2026

Bible Henyo ("the app") is an offline Bible word-guessing game in Tagalog and English, published by developer Manta Suy ("we", "us"), Philippines (package com.mantasuy.biblehenyo). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no in-app purchases, and no servers of our own. It does not request the Internet permission and does not send any data off your device.

Your game progress (stars and scores per level) and settings (language, sound, vibration, dark mode) are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. They are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. This data is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Links to jw.org

Each answer and clue has a Bible reference link to jw.org. When you tap one, the app opens it in your device's web browser; the app itself never loads web pages. Your use of jw.org is governed by that website's own terms and privacy policy, not by this policy.

Permissions

The app only uses the Vibrate permission, for short haptic feedback during the game (you can turn it off in Settings). It uses no other permissions.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Disclaimer

Bible Henyo is not an official jw.org app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by jw.org or the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society. All clues are original and written for this game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Patakaran sa Privacy (Tagalog)

Ang Bible Henyo ay offline na laro ng paghula ng salita mula sa Bibliya, gawa ng developer na Manta Suy (Pilipinas).

  • Walang kinokolektang personal na impormasyon. Walang account, walang ads, walang analytics, at walang sariling server. Hindi humihingi ang app ng Internet permission.
  • Ang iyong progreso at settings ay naka-save lang sa iyong device at mabubura kapag nag-clear data o nag-uninstall.
  • Ang mga link sa jw.org ay binubuksan sa browser ng iyong device, hindi sa loob ng app.
  • Vibrate lang ang permission na ginagamit, para sa maikling vibration habang naglalaro (puwedeng i-off sa Settings).
  • Hindi opisyal na app ng jw.org. Orihinal ang lahat ng clue na isinulat para sa larong ito.
