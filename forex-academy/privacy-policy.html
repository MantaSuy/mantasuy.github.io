Privacy Policy: Forex Indicator Academy

Developer: Manta Suy  |  App: com.mantasuy.forexacademy  |  Effective: October 26, 2026

Summary

Forex Indicator Academy is an educational trading simulator that uses virtual money. It does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Internet use

The app uses the Internet only to download public market prices (quotes and price candles) from biquote.io. These requests contain no personal information. As with any web request, the price provider's servers can see your device's IP address. That is covered by the provider's own privacy policy. The app does not send any identifier, account, or usage data.

Optional: if you choose to enter your own Twelve Data API key in Settings as a backup price source, the app sends price requests with that key to twelvedata.com. The key is stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your virtual balance, practice trades, indicator settings, drawings, quiz progress and preferences are saved only on your device (app local storage). They are never uploaded. You can delete them with "Reset demo balance" in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

No real trading

The app is not a broker and does not connect to any trading account. No payments or financial information are handled.

External links

Links (for example to license or data-provider pages) open in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
