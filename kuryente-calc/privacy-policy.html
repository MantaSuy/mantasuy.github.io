Privacy Policy: Kuryente Calculator PH

Developer: Manta Suy  |  App: com.mantasuy.kuryentecalc  |  Effective: October 26, 2026

Summary

Kuryente Calculator PH does not collect, store on servers, share, or sell any personal data. The app works fully offline and does not request the Internet permission.

Data stored on your device

Your appliance list, electricity rate, meter readings / bill history and solar settings are saved only on your device (app local storage). They are never sent anywhere. They are excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. You can delete them anytime using "I-reset lahat ng data" in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

No ads, analytics, or accounts

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and requires no account or sign-in.

External links

Some buttons (e.g. "Solar Guide PH") open another installed app or the Google Play Store. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page.

Tagalog / Taglish

Walang kinokolektang personal na data ang app. Lahat ng inilagay mo (appliances, rate, meter readings) ay naka-save lang sa phone mo at hindi ipinapadala kahit saan. Walang ads, walang tracking, walang account.
