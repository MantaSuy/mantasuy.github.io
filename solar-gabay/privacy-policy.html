Solar Guide PH – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

Solar Guide PH ("the app") is an offline, educational step-by-step guide to home solar PV installation in the Philippines, published by developer Manta Suy ("we", "us"), Philippines (package com.mantasuy.solargabay). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no servers of our own. It does not request the Internet permission and does not send any data off your device.

Your chosen system type and size, checklist progress, simulator choices, and the acceptance of the safety disclaimer are saved only on your device (in the app's local storage). They are removed when you use "I-reset ang progress", clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. This data is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.

Printing and PDF

When you choose to print or save the summary as PDF, the app hands the summary to Android's built-in print system. Where the file is saved or which printer is used is chosen by you; we never receive it.

External links

References and image credits link to third-party websites (for example Wikimedia Commons, Meralco, ERC). Tapping a link opens it in your own web browser, outside the app. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Safety disclaimer

The app is for education only. Electrical work is dangerous. System design, permits, and final connection must be done and signed off by a licensed Professional/Registered Electrical Engineer (PEE/REE) or licensed electrician.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.
