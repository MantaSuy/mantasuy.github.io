Notepad & Checklist

Privacy Policy · by Manta Suy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

In short: Notepad & Checklist (package com.mantasuy.notes), made by Manta Suy, does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access, no ads, no analytics and no accounts. Everything you create stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send your notes or any other information off your device. We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.

2. Data stored on your device

Your notes, checklists, labels, reminders, images, drawings, settings and your note-lock PIN are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are protected by Android's app sandbox and are not accessible to us.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • Microphone – only while you use voice-to-text. Speech recognition is performed by your phone's speech service (for example the Google app), which is governed by its provider's own privacy policy. The app receives only the recognized text and never records or stores audio.
  • Camera & photos – photos are taken with your system camera app and chosen with the Android photo picker. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device. Images are saved only inside the app.
  • Notifications, exact alarms & start at boot – to show reminders at the time you choose and to restore them after a restart.
  • Biometrics – to unlock locked notes with your fingerprint. Biometric data is handled entirely by Android; the app never has access to it.

4. Sharing

We do not share or sell any data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete notes in the app, clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Since we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Security

Data never leaves your device through the app. Locked notes require your PIN or fingerprint to open. Keep your device protected with a screen lock for best security.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
