Critter Blast – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Critter Blast: Bubble Shooter ("the app") is a bubble shooter game published by developer Manta Suy ("we", "us"), Philippines (package com.mantasuy.critterblast). This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information ourselves. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own. Your level progress (stars), coins, boosters, unlocked themes, daily reward status, purchase status and settings are saved only on your device (local storage). They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC: occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads and optional rewarded ads (for example to continue a level, refill a booster or double a reward). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) and app set ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • ad interaction data (ad views, clicks) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads

Personalized ads are shown only subject to your consent where required (collected through Google's User Messaging Platform, UMP); otherwise non-personalized ads may be shown. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers optional purchases: Remove Ads (stops interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads stay available), Jungle and Ocean themes, and coin packs. All payments are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The app receives from Google Play only what is needed to deliver and restore purchases (product, purchase token and status), used solely to unlock what you bought. Remove Ads and themes are restored automatically on the same Google account.

Links to Google Play

The "More Games" screen opens our other games on Google Play. Google Play's privacy policy applies there.

Your choices

  • Consent: Where required (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland), the app asks for consent through Google's consent message on first launch. You can change your choice later with "Privacy options" in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Delete data: Uninstall the app or clear its storage to delete all saved progress.

Children

Critter Blast is meant for teens and adults (13+). It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share personal information. Purchase information is handled by Google Play. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is sent to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; changes are posted on this page with a new date.
